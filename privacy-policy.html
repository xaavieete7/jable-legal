Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Jable only collects the data it needs to work. We don't sell your data, we don't share it for advertising, and we don't track you.

1. Data controller

Owner: Xavi Ete
Contact email: xavigrau09@gmail.com
App: Jable — Fuerteventura Beaches (iOS and Android)

2. Data we collect

Without an account: no personal data. You can use the map, check conditions and read reviews without signing up.

With an account:

  • Email address — to create your account and sign you in.
  • Name — the username you choose or, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the name that service shares. With Apple you can hide your real email.
  • User ID — generated by Supabase to link your data to your account.
  • Visited beaches — the ones you mark as visited.
  • Reviews — ratings, comments and the details you add to a review (water temperature, crowds, cleanliness, etc.).
  • Flag reports — the flag colour you report at a beach.
  • Achievements — the badges you unlock.

Location: the app only asks for it when you tap "My location" on the map, and uses it on your device to centre the map. It is never sent to our servers or linked to your account. If you deny the permission, the app works normally.

We don't use any analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service: your account, passport, visits, reviews and achievements.
  • To show beach information to the community (reviews and flags).
  • Essential account emails (for example, password resets).

4. Legal basis

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): data needed for the app to work.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): the location permission you grant on your device.

5. Where it is stored

In Supabase, on servers in the EU (Frankfurt, AWS eu-central-1), encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. See their policy at supabase.com/privacy.

6. How long we keep it

  • For as long as your account is active.
  • Flag reports expire after 24 hours.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account. Once you confirm, your account and all your data (profile, visits, reviews, reports and achievements) are deleted immediately and permanently. If you can't access the app, email us and we'll delete it for you.

8. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection and restriction by emailing xavigrau09@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your local authority.

9. Third-party services

  • Supabase — database and authentication.
  • Sign in with Apple / Google — if you use them to sign in, their privacy policies apply.
  • Open-Meteo — weather and marine forecasts. Only beach coordinates are sent, never your data.
  • Maps — Apple Maps on iOS and Google Maps on Android display the map and open "Directions". Apple's and Google's policies apply.
  • Expo (EAS Update) — delivers app updates; it receives no account data.

10. Children

Jable is not aimed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes

If we make significant changes, we'll let you know in the app. The "last updated" date always shows the current version.

12. Contact

xavigrau09@gmail.com
